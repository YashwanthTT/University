PYRAMID

About

The Great Pyramid of Giza is one of the most famous monuments in the world. Built over 4,500 years ago in Egypt, it is the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World and the only one still standing today.

Key Facts

  • Built around 2560 BCE.
  • Located in Giza, Egypt.
  • Built as a tomb for Pharaoh Khufu.
  • Original height was about 146.6 meters.
  • One of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
