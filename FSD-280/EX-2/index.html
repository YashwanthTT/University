About

The Great Pyramid of Giza is one of the most famous monuments in the world. Built over 4,500 years ago in Egypt, it is the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World and the only one still standing today.

Ancient Wonder

It is the oldest of the original Seven Wonders of the Ancient World and the only one still standing today.

Historical Facts

Built

Around 2560 BCE during the reign of Pharaoh Khufu.

Location

Giza Plateau, Egypt (near modern-day Cairo).

Purpose

Constructed as a monumental tomb for Pharaoh Khufu.

Dimensions

Original height was approximately 146.6 meters (481 feet).
